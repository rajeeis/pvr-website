Service Overview

Clean Setup. Stronger Guest Experience. Better Commercial Control.

PVR Groups Ltd helps hosts and property operators structure their serviced accommodation properly from the start. We focus on the parts that make the biggest difference: presentation, guest journey, pricing decisions, house rules, check-in flow, cleaning standards, deposits, reviews and monthly improvement.


This service is designed for people who want professional support, not generic advice. We give you a practical operating structure while keeping the strategy simple, controlled and commercially focused.

Best suited for

  • New short-stay hosts preparing their first unit
  • Existing hosts with poor occupancy or low profit
  • Property owners who want hands-off support
  • Operators needing better guest systems
  • Hosts needing stronger pricing and rules
  • Owners wanting a premium guest experience
For New & Existing Hosts

Support for People Building a Proper Short-Stay Business

Many beginners lose money because the property goes live before the pricing, photos, rules, messaging and guest process are ready. We help you avoid those mistakes and set up a cleaner, more professional operation.

Launch Planning

Guest-Facing Presentation

Pricing Structure

We help set base rates, minimum floors, weekend rules, last-minute logic, longer-stay discounts and seasonal positioning.

Guest Message Templates

We create polished messages for check-in, deposits, house rules, early check-in, late checkout, issue handling and review requests.

Operations Workflow

We structure cleaning, linen, key collection, damage checks, restocking and turnaround standards so the stay is smooth every time.

Performance Review

For existing hosts, we review weak points such as calendar gaps, low rates, poor conversions, unclear rules and missed long-stay opportunities.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help if I am completely new?

Yes. The Starter Review and Setup package are designed for beginners who need a professional structure before launch.

Can I run the property myself after setup?

Yes. Setup & Optimisation is for hosts who want to self-manage while using a cleaner, more professional structure.

Can you help an existing unit that is not performing?

Yes. We can review pricing, calendar gaps, presentation, guest rules, messaging and long-stay strategy.

Do you publicly reveal your full process?

No. Public pages explain the service clearly, but our internal systems and full operating playbook are shared only with suitable clients after enquiry.

Do you only work in London and Manchester?

Those are our main operating areas, but we can consider other UK locations where demand and operational support make sense.
