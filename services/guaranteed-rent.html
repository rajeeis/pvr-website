How We Operate

Professional Short and Medium-Term Accommodation

We provide well-managed accommodation for corporate occupiers, relocators, contractors, insurance-related stays, project teams and other professional guests who need flexible, high-standard housing.

We do not operate properties casually. Each suitable unit is treated as a professional accommodation asset, with guest communication, cleaning coordination, care standards and clear house rules.

Questions

Estate Agent FAQs

Is this suitable for every rental property?

No. We only consider properties where location, rent, access, condition and agreement terms make commercial sense.

Do you work directly with estate agents?

Yes. We welcome suitable property introductions from estate agents and aim to keep communication clear and professional.

How should agents explain this to landlords?

Position it as a professional company let arrangement with a registered property operator, strong property care standards and clear responsibilities.

Do you only work in London and Manchester?

London and Manchester are current focus areas, but we can consider strong locations across the UK where demand and numbers make sense.
