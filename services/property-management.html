Questions landlords ask

Clear answers before you hand over your property.

Why should I choose this instead of a regular tenant?

A regular tenant can be simpler, but income is normally fixed. Our managed route aims to create stronger commercial potential through guest-ready presentation, dynamic pricing, multi-channel demand and active management.

Will I still be kept updated?

Yes. The service is designed around transparent communication and monthly reporting, so landlords understand performance and recommended next steps.

Do you handle guests and cleaning?

Yes. Guest communication, check-in instructions, cleaning coordination and operational issue handling are part of the managed service.

Is every property suitable?

No. Suitability depends on location, demand, building rules, lease terms, condition, access and expected numbers. That is why we start with a review.

What areas do you cover?

Our main focus is London and Manchester, but we review strong UK hotspot locations where guest demand and operations make sense.
