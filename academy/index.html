Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Deal Sourcing Academy.

Who is this academy for?
The academy is designed for property investors, estate agents, developers, and anyone interested in mastering deal sourcing. No prior experience is required — we start from fundamentals and progress to advanced scenarios.
Do I need property experience?
No. The academy is structured to start with core deal sourcing foundations, then build toward more advanced investment analysis and deal packaging.
Are templates included?
Yes. Templates and worksheets are included to help you review opportunities, structure your analysis and present deals more professionally.
Is support included?
Support depends on the access tier. Priority members will be notified first when full launch details and support options are confirmed.
Can I access future updates?
Yes. Academy access is designed to include future updates so members can keep learning as strategies, templates and case studies evolve.
What's the time commitment?
The video content totals 20+ hours. You can consume it at your own pace — some people complete it in 3 weeks, others prefer spreading it over several months. Access never expires, so you learn on your schedule.
Do I get lifetime access?
Yes. Once you enrol, you have lifetime access to the course materials and all future updates. This is included in all three pricing tiers.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
We're confident you'll find value in the academy. If you're unsatisfied within 14 days, we offer a full refund, no questions asked.
What's included in the Pro tier?
Pro includes all 4 modules (20+ hours of video), investment memo templates, financial modelling spreadsheets, and real case studies. It's our most popular option for serious deal sourcing professionals.
What additional support do VIP members receive?
VIP members get access to our private investor network, monthly live Q&A sessions with industry experts, personalized deal review consultations, and a one-on-one strategy call to kickstart your sourcing plan.
Can I upgrade my tier later?
Absolutely. If you start with Starter and decide to upgrade to Pro or VIP, we'll credit your initial payment toward the upgrade price. Simply contact us to arrange it.
How do the payment systems work?
Enrolment and payments are currently being configured. Please use the Enrol buttons above to express your interest, and we'll guide you through the enrollment process.
